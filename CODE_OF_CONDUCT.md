# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in PRISM's community respectful and harassment-free, regardless of identity, background, experience level or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas, code and evidence rather than people.
- Protect private data, credentials and unpublished materials.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes.
- Respect contributor boundaries and project safety rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information, credentials, corpus materials or prompts without permission.
- Misrepresenting experimental or partial results as established facts.
- Deliberately bypassing project security, evidence or access-control boundaries.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately under the process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may request corrections, remove content, limit participation or take other proportionate action to protect contributors and the project.
